# STEER Glossary

_Framework v3.0 · operational language_

## Intent

An intent is a candidate brief in the intent backlog. It has not consumed pod capacity and is not a work item until a Product Lead pulls it into flight.

## Pull

Pull is the Product Lead's deliberate commitment of a candidate intent into flight against the pod's visible WIP limit. It is the boundary where a candidate becomes a work item.

## Work item

A work item is an intent that a Product Lead has pulled into flight. It carries the continuous artifact chain, evidence, signatures, aging, and release record.

## Exam

The exam is the independently authored, binding definition of provably done: acceptance tests, evaluations, and guardrails. Builders can never edit it.

## Gate

A gate is one of three human signature points: Gate 1 accepts intent and design, Gate 2 accepts the exam, and Gate 3 judges whether evidence supports release.

## Band

A band is the historical cycle-time range for a Flight Board state. A breach escalates to a huddle rather than creating routine progress notifications.

## Outcome contract

An outcome contract states the measurable production change a brief intends to cause, including its baseline, denominator, target, observation window, and guardrail.

## Default-open

A default-open domain may proceed when its machine checks are green and the ordinary gate accountabilities have signed.

## Default-closed

A default-closed domain blocks until its conditional specialist seat has signed. Accessibility, security, privacy, money, legal, and irreversible operations may activate this route.
