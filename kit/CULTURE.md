# STEER culture: the human channel

This file is versioned because culture erodes at machine speed when it is not
made explicit.

## Two rooms by design

Work rooms with agents are signed and on the record. Every pod also protects a
human-only room and one synchronous ritual. Decisions stay on the record;
doubts are welcome off it.

## Blameless by construction

Audit artifacts and decisions, never individual fallibility. A gate signature
must not be used against its signer when the agreed process was followed.

## Cohesion is scheduled work

The huddle and learning review are part of delivery. Product Lead attention is
a named capacity risk, not an unlimited queue.

## Learning happens in public

Judgment is shared so the next generation of decision owners can grow.
