# Providing intent

Humans provide judgment; agents provide document structure. The originator
answers a plain-language interview and corrects a rendered draft. Git,
Markdown, and raw templates remain behind the intent-home seam.

## Governing rules

1. Interview backward from the template; do not expose a form as the primary
   experience.
2. Never invent. Put every unstated or unverified fact in Open Questions.
3. Surface ambiguity instead of smoothing it over.
4. After the same correction appears twice, propose a versioned context update.
5. Resolve system names against real organizational context before committing.

## Stage modes

- Brief: interview toward completeness.
- Spec: dialogue over two or three options and their trade-offs.
- Exam: present executable cases as meaning for human judgment.
- Plan: let the engineer interrogate files, sequence, and risks before dispatch.

Save commits the accepted rendered draft under the human originator's identity.
All entry points normalize into one intent home.
