### Immediate-trigger follow-on design constraints

The existing complete verifier already selects the earliest supersession/rebuild
request and binds that trigger into every human decision. Its existing tests verify
both event orderings and reject authority bound to the later trigger. Preserve those
semantics when constructing the source's exact +6-second complete case.

The source's before observation occurs one second before its supplied trigger, so
that future trigger cannot be treated as an observed event. A pre-trigger read-only
result needs an actually available, signed complete history/state with no qualifying
trigger. It must say waiting-for-trigger, not compute expiry from an unobserved event.
At/after observations need available head-only evidence and pending disposition,
not future action receipts or a claim of instantaneous physical deletion.

The current readiness entry point deliberately does not admit RC-REBUILDABLE.
Any additional immediate-state surface must be explicit and separately pinned;
keep existing readiness policies, envelopes and observations unchanged and prove
no cross-format or extra-argument path can enable mutation or weaken full checks.
This is the next design constraint, not a claim that the surface is implemented.
