# Brief

The frozen event keys expire in 2027, before several one-, three- and seven-year
retention boundaries. Extending their windows would hide this gap. Introduce a
separate fact-only history revalidation contract with trusted current keys and
complete retained bytes, while leaving current action-authority checks strict.

No approved policy, original signature, source event or frozen registry changes.
This candidate designs evidence verification; it does not authorize new access,
attestations by real parties, records deletion, spending or deployment.
