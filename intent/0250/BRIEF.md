# Why

Valid citations and complete batches do not establish that duplicate/overlap
judgments are correct. Before authorizing live model testing, define reproducible
quality criteria and prove that the evaluator detects structurally valid wrong
answers, omitted scope and uncertain inventory.

Deliver an offline production-verifier replay harness with a candidate synthetic
corpus, exact bindings and honest failure denominators. Keep reference answers out
of model input. Do not change production prompts, backend activation or authority.
