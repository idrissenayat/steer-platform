# Do not drop assessed scope at the drafting handoff

A collection of 34 short documents can fit the existing drafting-byte allowance
yet fail solely because the old envelope admits 32 documents. The new path must
carry every whole source, including exclusions and Briefs outside that old subset,
after exact recorded assessment and explicit human direction.

Do not solve size pressure by increasing spending authority, discarding qualifiers,
calling partial search complete or compressing sources into unverified summaries.
Collections that exceed the unchanged content limits remain explicitly blocked.
