# Brief — Repository-wide intent evidence

Duplicate/overlap review cannot rely on a configured item list. Newly added items,
legacy intents and pending amendments must be discoverable at the same revision,
without treating proposed scope as accepted canonical work.

Add bounded namespace enumeration and a read-only evidence composition consuming
independently verified product access and lifecycle selection. Preserve whole
Brief/Spec context, exact hashes and missing coverage. Connect the existing review
API without enabling live services or claiming semantic duplicate assessment.
