# Development acceptance

Not a protected Exam or independent gate decision.

- Canonical gate bytes and unchanged artifacts are verified before and after signers.
- Every declared signer matches a unique ordered configured subject/hat and exact facts.
- Missing, additional, reordered or substituted signatures cannot form a partial pass.
- Every signer invokes the actual provider/identity/qualification implementation.
- Derived qualified domains cannot come from an unsupported canonical assertion.
- Send-back remains send-back; normalized evidence never becomes gate/write authority.
- Source movement, actor changes, backward time and earliest service expiry deny.
- Real timeout keeps single-flight ownership and draining shutdown without late reads.
- Native Git and genuine ephemeral signatures exercise the composition, not callbacks.
- Full checks pass and protected artifacts, real approvals and permissions are unchanged.
