# Carry the review into drafting

A person should not review duplicate/overlap findings and then have drafting lose
that context. Preserve the exact assessment and their explicit direction with the
saved source revision. Draft Brief and Spec from those inputs; give the separate
Test Agent the same source and direction plus the new Brief/Spec, never an old Exam.

Only an explicitly complete empty inventory avoids an assessment call. Missing,
stale, failed or incomplete search is not an empty corpus or evidence of novelty.
Findings inform the human; no finding chooses a direction, merges work or saves.
