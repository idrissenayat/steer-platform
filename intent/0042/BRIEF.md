# Brief

A durable watch needs actual source observations instead of a synthetic port.
Compare the explicitly governed artifact set at its target revision against one
current Git commit, locate its configured gate record through a complete bounded
inventory, and emit only provenance references after fresh authorization.

Keep record format/provenance distinct from signer eligibility and approval
policy. A send-back record must be observable without becoming approval. Source
outages, removed artifacts, integrity failures and revoked observer grants must
never produce a successful fallback from cached state.
