# Brief

Connect the internal fixed-failed-run recovery primitive to a portable, separately
authorized command/status contract and an owned one-attempt client. Preserve ordinary
dispatch duplicate rejection, the original save operation and current projector checks.
A lost start acknowledgment must lead to exact status inspection, not another start.

Do not enable live recovery or treat save, dispatch, ingestion, human hats or a
passing test as recovery authority. Identity runtime ownership and approved live
bindings remain follow-on work.
