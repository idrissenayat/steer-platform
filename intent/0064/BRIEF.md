# Brief

## Intent

Complete explicit signature-time integration for the remaining cost modes and
the spending-decision evidence consumer under R5-002. Preserve exact arithmetic,
authorization lineage and historical records without enabling a money operation.

## Outcome

Every chain link, nested provider proof, consumer, replay snapshot, CAS head,
reservation, price, usage, invoice and ledger line is verified at a named time
and the trusted evaluation time. Replay cannot bypass reservation timing.
Forecast/spend authority must be current; historical invoice/aggregate audits
remain distinct from new spending requests.

## Boundaries

Independent observations attest exact supplied bytes, not original issuance of
legacy records lacking timestamps. Successful audit is not execution authority,
an actual payment, a verified stored replay-result payload or live CAS execution.
No spending, provider access, deployment, release, protected edits or signatures.
