# Development acceptance

Not a protected Exam or independent gate decision.

- Genuine qualification signatures bind the exact human, identity issuer and scope.
- Authorities cannot attest domains outside their authorized scope, and required
  domains cannot come from caller-supplied qualifications or a specialist hat alone.
- Qualification is recorded by signing time and valid both then and at evaluation.
- Current qualification/key revocation, expiry or moved source head denies.
- All three proofs and both role sources are required; older modes never upgrade.
- Source corruption, encoding/size violations, path collisions and missing sources deny.
- All modes share admission/deadline/shutdown; final-clock checks cover qualifications.
- Native Git exercises actual history and current proofs followed by role revocation.
- The actual policy evaluator accepts the derived specialist domains among synthetic
  remaining facts, rejects missing domains and still requires source verification.
- Full checks pass and protected sources, live permissions and real approvals stay unchanged.
