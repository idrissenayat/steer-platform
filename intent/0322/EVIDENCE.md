Only the exact API-constructed current scope reader, pinned method and scope
can lend its existing records/key lease to a final review. Unknown and wrapped
readers retain the prior path. A lazy bridge opens that lease only at the first
actual assessment read, after dependent preview authorization, and retains the
producer through final native comparison and real drainage. The shared phase encloses initial/repeated/final
assessment consumption inside the source-review owner, without admission,
persistence or scheduling. Full native scope comparison precedes the final draft
check and enclosing source/corpus closure. Each side of an effect remains separate.

The projection helper runs independent metadata-purpose queries and then the exact
caller. An identical caller is not invoked twice at the same no-IO reuse boundary;
different or bound callbacks still run between both fresh owner checks. Selected
record and key purposes stay live through final key/SQL IO. No authorization or
grant result is cached. Port replacement, malformed/repeated callbacks and late
source/draft/records/key/caller changes fail closed. Actual pending work drains.
