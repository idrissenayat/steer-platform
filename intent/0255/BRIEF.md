# Brief

People must be able to inspect what an earlier scope assessment actually said
after its execution window expires or the draft changes. Current-only access
cannot be broadened into a hidden renewal of model or save permission.

Provide a separate current-authority historical read and a clearly labeled
read-only view in the real editor. Keep retained source citations, incomplete
coverage and uncertainty visible. Never replace current text or let historical
evidence unlock current direction, generation, saving or gate controls.
