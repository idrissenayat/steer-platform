its complete retained child evidence, with no omitted, duplicated or substituted
children. Require a fresh complete manifest and independent authoritative history
head after archive retention. Missing evidence must never mean an empty corpus.

decision. Existing qualified-event/archived-owner profiles cover hold decisions,
not that decision. This increment verifies metadata/history/manifest consistency
and a boundary candidate only, leaving retirement authority explicit and false.
