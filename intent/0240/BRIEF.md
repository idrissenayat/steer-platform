# Brief

The platform must not mistake one finished duplicate-review batch for a complete
review of the permitted existing scope. After restart it needs to recover the
successful saved responses, preserve pending or unresolved work and explain any
coverage gaps without another model send.

Build a read-only composition against the encrypted original corpus and durable
checkpoints. Revalidate exact source bindings, response verification and current
identity/records/key authority before releasing findings. Keep later human edits
separate and show expiration without renewing old execution authority.

This increment does not claim semantic accuracy, uniqueness, current write grants,
gate approval, live provider acceptance or a completed human UI workflow.
