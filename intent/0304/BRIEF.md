# Owned current and historical corpus graph

Connect 0303's real GitHub batch reader to a production-source, owned collector
for exact current/historical corpus revisions. Read roots/pointers, manifests and
documents in dependency order; keep canonical, candidate and amendment semantics,
independent source permissions and final closure after dependent records readback.
Keep the existing application factory unchanged until the combined records and
outer-effect composition is complete. This is not a separate preview, a new stack,
records adoption or a completed performance/UI checkpoint.
