# Owned native corpus graph: verified composition, not installed workflow

The production-source collector now connects 0303's native batch adapter to exact
current and historical corpus revisions. It traverses roots/pointers, manifests
and all three bundle documents in dependency order, preserving canonical/candidate/
amendment semantics and exact original bytes. Every revision/path has its own grants.
Missing, inaccessible, unresolved, nonregular or corrupt sources reject the whole
graph; only explicitly out-of-product roots are excluded. Output names its scope
and explicitly does not establish authoritative newness.

The owner admits at most four calls. Cancellation or close promptly withholds a
result, but does not free a slot while the actual callback or IO remains pending.
Shutdown waits for those operations. The 30-second monotonic lifetime rejects
expiry, invalid times and backward clocks. A trusted read-only dependent callback
finishes before final selection, every consumed-source grant, caller/all-grants
revision and head rechecks. No model dispatch or save belongs inside that callback.
