# Brief

A typed, provider-signed hold event alone does not establish that its author is
the qualified privacy/legal records owner. A separately verified owner decision
also does not establish its connection to a particular committed event.

Join both proofs at the actual history boundary: exact actor and hat, event bytes,
record selector, preceding hold, approval timing and unique decision identities.
Preserve independent, later disposition/tombstone approvals. Retain historical
hold applications as restrictive facts; do not invent retroactive authority for
historical releases. Do not edit frozen policy, Exam or gate artifacts.
