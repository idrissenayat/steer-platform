# Brief

## Intent

Complete the named accessibility timing path and make the full public-oracle
coverage inventory explicit, without presenting synthetic verification as a
manual accessibility result or changing protected source records.

## Outcome

All six signed records have explicit native or independently observed-as-of
time bases and a trusted evaluation clock. Every raw row must fit within the
reviewer's identity/qualification/assignment interval and precede the signed
summary. The exact original matrix, raw digest and outcome checks still apply.
Unverified metadata fails before row consumption; row failures close iteration.

## Boundaries

No actual accessibility session, qualified-human approval, production evidence
collection or provider call is performed. Candidate success always reports
manualAuditComplete false and executionAuthorized false. Full normative coverage,
long-retention/migration work and independent/protected acceptance remain open.
