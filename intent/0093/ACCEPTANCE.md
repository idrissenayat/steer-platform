# Development acceptance · not a protected EXAM

- Pending and complete chains verify exact stored/current checkpoint evidence
  after delivered or lost acknowledgment, without another effect or resume grant.
- All twelve signed records and their distinct trust-domain roles are mandatory.
- Progress, next step, original chain/evidence and retained object pins are exact.
- Opening winner, terminal +1 head/consumption and current non-winning readback
  retain exact request, retention, result and predecessor lineage.
- Independent transport evidence and later readback remain required after loss.
- Original/current full revalidation rejects expired plan or human evidence;
  original signed bytes are preserved and exact expiry/chronology never round.
- Wrong scope, unsafe object paths, legacy versions, extra fields, omitted
  evidence and malformed or absent trusted clocks deny with zero effects.

No actual durable store, chain-wide head resolution, restart or gate acceptance.
