# Acceptance boundary

Development checks must preserve full Brief/Spec/amendment context in bounded
batches; withhold incomplete targets; bind every batch to its parent source/scope;
reject edited plans, wrong profiles, invented citations and substituted receipts;
and keep missing, inaccessible, abstained or unplanned work incomplete.

The real review service and production React component must exchange/display the
verified plan without changing execution eligibility. Fifty provided documents
can form multiple planned batches, not a falsely complete legacy first-32 review.

Synthetic findings and citations test structure only. They do not demonstrate
paraphrase/negation/partial-overlap accuracy, model quality, current real permissions,
approved records activation, provider spending or human UI/save acceptance.
This document is development acceptance criteria, not the canonical Exam or a gate.
