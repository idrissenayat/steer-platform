# 0318 — Joint current-scope/original phase contract

1. The explicit owned original-read hook supplies a private current-scope port.
   The data owner continues canonical original validation and exact recorded-scope
   binding on every consumption. Missing, foreign, historical and expired scope
   cannot satisfy a current recorded original. Ordinary fallback order is unchanged.
2. One current-scope window encloses dependent original reads, start authorization,
   operation inspection and complete final development records/key comparison.
   Its initial/current result is immutable; its full final read must equal that
   result and runs after final development records/key comparison.
3. Final scope IO and return freshly re-grant development record/key purposes,
   original/source, draft and operation authority. The full original records and
   key check is not replaced by hashes, cached permission or a scope-only check.
4. The scope reader, subject and methods remain pinned; changed source, result,
   scope status, record/key permission or caller authority denies before scheduling.
   Escaped/overlapping/skipped reads and early/nonvoid hook completion still deny.
5. Scheduling remains outside each phase. Scheduler-requested and post-effect
   validation open new phases; late denial withholds acknowledgement without
   dispatching again. Closing a starter while final scope IO is held retains its
   owned admission until the actual work drains.
6. Measure all provider attempts in both native synthetic authenticated journey
   directions. Do not raise C22 limits or claim p95/UI/live quality from undelayed
   synthetic samples. Report 68% (17/25; +0) until a full checkpoint is verified.
7. Immutable intermediate scope consumption uses one fresh source/caller barrier
   before a synchronous return. Full initial/final reads keep their complete IO
   brackets. Do not execute two identical permission queries with no intervening
   IO, but never reuse a prior permission decision or omit per-consumption checks.

Verification requires focused owner/boundary checks, native ordinary/owned start,
both synthetic save/recovery/reopen directions, adversarial cross-boundary tests,
types and broad regressions. No deployment, model call or runtime GitHub write.
