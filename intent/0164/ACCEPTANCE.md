- Source is bound to the exact selected Brief, decision and recorded artifact reference.
- Independent curation and all current grants remain mandatory before source disclosure.
- Changed revisions, malformed/corrupt data and revoked identity fail closed.

- Selection/close/refresh/lifecycle handling clears source and preserves keyboard use.
- Isolated integrated/browser and full checks pass; boundaries and observed limits are documented.
- Delivery requires post-commit remote equality verification in the task record.
