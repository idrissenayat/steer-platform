# Brief

The production workspace has no access to STEER's operating guidance, even though
the prototype already consumes the eight-document kit manifest. Members should be
able to read that same canon without returning to the prototype or an external file.

Provide a read-only Learn reader with a document list, persistent desktop outline,
local section search, kit version and exact source fingerprints. Preserve the
pink/orange visual system and do not make a second editable knowledge store.

This implements the reading slice of intent/0004 and delivery milestone M5.
Live writing, authentication bootstrap, gates, orientation ending in real actions,
glossary peeks, instrumentation and qualified accessibility acceptance are outside
this increment. Their existing requirements remain open.
