# Acceptance boundary

Synthetic HTTP/MCP/React and disposable PostgreSQL/native-Git checks can prove
that exact original requests are restored and inspected without another write,
and that a verified receipt links to its exact bundle. They do not establish
live human acceptance or authorize records, model spending or real saving.

I5/I6 stay open pending current source/lifecycle/write authority, actual human
preparation/confirmation/start and a demonstrated signed-in saved-repository
journey. No observed committed receipt is a gate signature, quarantine release,
retry grant or completed-workflow claim. User-owned files and signed requirements
remain untouched.
