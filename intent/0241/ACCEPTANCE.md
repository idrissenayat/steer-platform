and the pinned SDK verifier without another reservation, model call or SQL write.

false-ready/clearance states, altered result digests, invalid source/citation
accounting, absent services/grants, changed identities, mismatched current profiles,
non-void source authority and private error leakage. Incomplete coverage and
unresolved work remain explicit; corrections supersede and expiry exposes metadata.

approval, verified real source grant or actual signed-in UI/save acceptance.
