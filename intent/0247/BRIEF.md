# Brief — Recover scope review without replacement work

A human returning to a saved draft needs to find a retained overlap assessment,
read progress or findings, and explicitly recover a pending workflow if necessary.
Losing browser state must not require remembering a UUID or buying another run.

Preserve free-text entry, saved/unsaved source, authentication, tenant boundaries,
pink/orange controls and explicit human direction. No discovery response may claim
that the intent is new, a review is semantically correct or execution is authorized.

Current scope is the exact latest saved draft under the owner's current records
configuration. Historical draft revisions, orphaned pre-capture admissions and
historical-corpus finding display are outside this increment, not silently absent.
