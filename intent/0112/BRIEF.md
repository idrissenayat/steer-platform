# Brief

Before/complete mappings cannot prove the state at expiry when completed provider
receipts do not yet exist. Supplying future receipts or treating missing evidence
as a successful deletion would misstate that state. Add a separate read-only
retention-eligibility surface using authentic, fresh head evidence, with explicit
remaining requirements and no action authority. Preserve complete disposition checks.

Map eighteen exact at/after coordinates for the nine supported classes. Keep raw,
indefinite, immediate and other unsupported classes outside this bounded profile.
